Your best patients are already on your list.

Every clinic has patients who loved their results and simply never rebooked. Slotboost turns that list into appointments, without adding work for your front desk.

Find who has lapsed

Upload your patient list and Slotboost shows who is overdue, based on their last visit and treatment. Botox patients and filler patients lapse on different timelines, so the rules differ by treatment.

Reach them the right way

Short, friendly texts and emails go out only to patients who can be contacted: consent on record, quiet hours respected, STOP honoured instantly, and nobody messaged twice.

See what it brought back

Every message carries a booking link, and your dashboard leads with patients who came back and the revenue they brought. Confirmed and estimated figures are kept separate, so you can trust the number.

Set up in an afternoon. No new system to learn.

Slotboost works alongside the booking software you already use. You don't need to change how your team works.

  • Upload your list. Export patients from your booking system as a spreadsheet and drop it in. Your column layout is remembered for next time.
  • Approve the campaign. Preview who will be contacted and exactly what they will receive before anything is sent.
  • Watch patients return. Replies land in one simple inbox, bookings are tracked, and revenue recovered is counted for you.
