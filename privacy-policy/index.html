Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains how Slotboost.io ("Slotboost", "we", "us") handles personal information. Slotboost.io is operated by Alethara Ltd, 128 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX, UK. It covers two situations: visitors to our website, and the patient information we process on behalf of clinics that use our service.

1. Who is responsible for your information

For information you give us directly on this website, such as joining our waitlist, Alethara Ltd is the data controller.

For patient information that a clinic uploads to or connects with Slotboost, the clinic decides why and how that information is used, and Slotboost acts as its service provider. Where the clinic is a HIPAA covered entity, we act as its business associate and sign a business associate agreement with it. If you are a patient and have a question about your information, please contact your clinic first.

2. Information we collect

From website visitors and waitlist sign-ups:

  • Your name, email address and any other details you enter in our waitlist form.
  • Messages you send us by email.
  • Basic technical information that our hosting and security systems record automatically, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested.

From clinics using the service, about their patients:

  • Contact details, such as name, phone number and email address.
  • Appointment and visit history, such as dates, treatment categories and amounts paid.
  • Communication consent records, opt-outs, and message delivery and reply history.

We only process patient information to provide the service to the clinic that supplied it.

3. How we use information

  • To run the waitlist and email you about early access and founding-member pricing.
  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To operate, secure and improve our website and service.
  • To provide the service to clinics: identifying patients who have not returned, sending consent-checked texts and emails on the clinic's behalf, honouring opt-outs such as STOP, and reporting the revenue recovered.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use patient information for our own marketing or share it with third parties for their marketing.

4. Legal bases (UK and EEA visitors)

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, we rely on your consent (for example, when you join our waitlist), our legitimate interests in running and securing our business, performance of a contract, and compliance with legal obligations.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run Slotboost, and only as needed for that purpose. These include providers of website form handling and email delivery, hosting and infrastructure, and text and email messaging. Each is required to protect the information and use it only for the services it provides to us. Where patient information is involved, these providers are bound by appropriate agreements.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of our business.

6. International transfers

We are based in the UK and our service is built for US clinics, so information may be processed in the United Kingdom and the United States. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep information

We keep waitlist information until you ask us to remove it or until it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for. Patient information is kept for as long as the clinic uses Slotboost and is deleted or returned at the clinic's direction when the relationship ends, subject to any legal retention requirements.

8. Security

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, access controls and monitoring. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). Residents of some US states have similar rights under state privacy laws.

To exercise these rights for information we hold as a controller, email us at joseph@slotboost.io. For patient information held on behalf of a clinic, please contact the clinic directly, and we will help them respond to your request.

10. Text and email messages

Messages sent through Slotboost are sent on behalf of a clinic and only to patients who can be contacted under the clinic's consent records. You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP, and of emails by using the unsubscribe link or contacting the clinic. Opt-outs are applied immediately.

11. Children

Our website and service are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children through our website.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. If the changes are significant, we will take reasonable steps to let you know.

13. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle personal information, email joseph@slotboost.io or write to Alethara Ltd, 128 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX, UK.
